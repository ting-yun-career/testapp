FAQ

Questions parents ask

Do you teach in person or online?

Both — whichever suits your family. Sessions can also mix formats week to week.

What if my child is falling behind?

I slow down. More story breaks, more hands-on exercises, no judgment — just steady progress at the pace they can actually absorb.

What if my child is ahead and wants more challenge?

I speed up — harder problems, AP-level material, and more independent work sooner.

Do you cover AP Physics?

Yes, alongside BC Physics 11/12 — many students study both together.

Can you help with college and university applications?

Yes — I can help with course selection, timelines, and the application itself.

Can you teach other subjects like math, chemistry, or computer programming?

Physics is my specialty and where I can add the most value. That said, I'm happy to help with the math that comes up alongside physics, and can occasionally support chemistry or computer programming on request — reach out and we can talk through what your student needs.
